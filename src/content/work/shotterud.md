### Need to kick off your pregame?

The game let's the host add people/groups/categories and set a min/max time. A random amount of roulette spinners will start spinning at random between the minimum and maximum time. Anyone selected by the spinner, or belonging to the selected group/category must make a toast to the entire gathering saying "SHOTTERUD".
